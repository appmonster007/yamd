# More widgets

- **Calendar:** opens on the next month with an event; previous, next and today buttons; click a day or week to narrow the list under the grid.
- **Timeline:** a month scale, lanes from headings, bars from `start` and `due`; the schedule is listed underneath.
- **Gallery:** a list of `- ![caption](image.png)`. **Choose images…** opens a file picker, and clicking an image enlarges it. A `source="folder/"` shows a whole folder.

Select a task list and run **Turn Selection into a Widget** to make a board, calendar or timeline from it.
