# Study tools

- **Study This Note** turns the note's definitions, theorems, lemmas and questions into `> [!card]` flashcards in a generated region, then starts a review.
- Write a card by hand: `> [!card] Question?` with the answer on the next lines.
- **Review Cards** schedules reviews with SM-2; the schedule is saved in `.yamd/srs.json`.
- Tasks with `{due=YYYY-MM-DD}` appear under **Deadlines**. A once-a-day reminder lists cards due and deadlines today or overdue (`yamd.study.reminders`).
- **Start Study Timer** and the **Dashboard** track time and progress by course.
