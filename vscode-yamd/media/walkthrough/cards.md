# Add and edit without typing Markdown

- **Add Card**, **Add Event** and **Add Timeline Item** ask for the column or date in a quick pick, then add the card. They also appear as a second link above each block.
- In a panel, the **pencil** on a card opens a form: due date, owner, tags and notes. Saving rewrites only that card's lines.
- The **filter box** hides cards that don't match: text, `@owner` or `#tag`.
- Anything not done and past its date shows **in red**.

Calendar and timeline cards use `{due=YYYY-MM-DD}` and, for the timeline, `{start=YYYY-MM-DD}`.
