# One menu for everything

**YAMD: Menu** groups the commands: write, widgets, data, study and share. You don't have to remember command names; pick what you want to do.

- The **YAMD** item in the status bar (bottom left) opens the same menu. Turn it off with the `yamd.statusBar.enabled` setting.
- The **YAMD** icon in the activity bar lists the widgets and structure of the open note, your flashcards, deadlines and study progress.
- Every action above a widget block (the links called CodeLens) is also in the Command Palette.
