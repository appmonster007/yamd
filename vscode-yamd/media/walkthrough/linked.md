# Linked notes

Write `[[another-note]]` to link notes. **Show Backlinks**, **Show Note Graph** and **Show Tags** open Foam's views of how your notes connect.

YAMD installs its companions with it: Foam, SQLite3 Editor, Edit CSV, Rainbow CSV and Markdown All in One. **Open in CSV Editor** and **Open in SQLite Editor** (on a query's source file) hand data files to them.
